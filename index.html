FAQ

An AML (Anti-Money Laundering) check analyzes a wallet address against known risk sources: sanctions lists, darknet markets, mixers, scam reports, and suspicious transaction patterns. It helps assess whether funds may be linked to illicit activity.
The current version runs in demo mode and is free for testing. For production-grade results with live blockchain analytics data, you will need to connect a licensed AML data provider API.
